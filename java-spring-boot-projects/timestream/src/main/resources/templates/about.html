About Us

Welcome to TimeStream — a Doctor Who–inspired chat app where every conversation is its own little timeline. Start a chat, jump between sessions, and leave messages like you’re dropping notes across time and space.

What is TimeStream?

TimeStream is a simple chat experience built around sessions (think: “episodes”). Each session keeps its own message history, so you can bounce between timelines without losing the plot.

How it works

  • Create a chat to open a brand-new timeline.
  • View all chats to revisit older timelines whenever you want.
  • Customize your profile — because even Time Lords update their look sometimes.

Doctor Who vibes (without the chaos)

  • Regeneration Aesthetics: Pick a Doctor theme to change the feel of your profile — like a UI regeneration cycle (no exploding TARDIS required).
  • Timeline Tracker: See how long you’ve been “traveling” since your account was created.
  • Open Chats Counter: Keep an eye on how many timelines you currently have open.

House rules (very important)

  • Don’t blink. (But seriously: keep your chats respectful.)
  • No Dalek behavior. Exterminate negativity somewhere else.
  • Be kind. Because kindness is cool. (And brave.)

Whether you’re here to chat casually or organize conversations into neat little timelines, TimeStream is built to keep things fun, clean, and a little bit wibbly-wobbly.
